This Privacy Policy explains how Infinity Pulse Studios ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in ARXO: Arrow Puzzles ("ARXO" or the "App").

ARXO does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, telephone number, postal address, or other directly identifying information to play.

ARXO may use third-party services for advertising, app distribution, and in-app purchases. Those services may process device and usage information under their own privacy policies.

1. Data We Collect

Information you provide

ARXO does not have user accounts or profile registration. We do not intentionally collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location through the App.

Information generated by the App

ARXO may store game progress and settings locally on your device, such as level progress, virtual coin balance, power-up balances, audio settings, vibration settings, and purchased entitlements. These are used to operate the game and are not intended to identify you personally.

Internet and device connectivity

The App checks whether an internet connection is available so that online gameplay and online services can function. The connectivity check does not intentionally collect your precise location or contacts.

2. How We Use Information

Information handled by ARXO and its service providers may be used to:

  • provide and maintain gameplay and game progression;
  • remember settings and purchased entitlements;
  • process and validate in-app purchases;
  • deliver advertisements and measure advertising performance;
  • diagnose crashes, errors, fraud, abuse, or technical problems; and
  • protect the security and integrity of the App and its services.

3. Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do not sell your personal information.

ARXO may share or make information available to service providers that are necessary to operate the App, including:

  • Google Play, for app distribution, purchase processing, licensing, and related fraud/security functions;
  • advertising providers, for serving ads and measuring ad performance; and
  • technical/service providers where reasonably necessary to maintain, secure, or troubleshoot the App.

These providers may process information such as device identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, device information, app interaction data, diagnostics, and purchase or transaction information, depending on the service and the features enabled in the App.

Third-party providers process information according to their own policies and applicable law.

4. Advertising

ARXO may display banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements.

Advertising providers may collect or receive device and usage information to deliver, limit, personalize, measure, and protect advertising. The specific data processed depends on the advertising SDKs and partners enabled in the released version of ARXO.

Users who purchase the Remove Ads entitlement will have advertising disabled in accordance with the App's functionality.

5. In-App Purchases

ARXO offers optional one-time purchases, including virtual coin packs and permanent entitlements such as Offline Play and Remove Ads.

Payments are processed through Google Play. We do not receive or store your full payment-card number or bank-account credentials through ARXO.

ARXO may receive purchase-related information from Google Play that is necessary to grant and restore the purchased item or entitlement, prevent abuse, and maintain purchase state.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information handled by the App and its service providers. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Game progress and settings stored locally remain on your device until they are cleared, the App is uninstalled, or the relevant in-App reset function changes them.

Data handled by third-party providers is retained according to their respective policies and legal requirements.

Because ARXO does not provide user accounts, we do not maintain an account database that users can log into or request to delete. For privacy questions or requests concerning information handled by Infinity Pulse Studios, contact us using the address below.

8. Children's Privacy

ARXO is not designed to require children to submit personal information. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through account registration because ARXO does not provide account registration.

Where required by law or platform rules, advertising and other third-party services may apply additional protections or restrictions.

9. International Processing

Google Play and other service providers may process information on servers located in countries other than the country where you live. By using ARXO, you acknowledge that such processing may occur subject to applicable law and the providers' privacy policies.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when ARXO's features, service providers, or legal requirements change. The updated policy will be published at this URL with a revised effective date.
